# Vault Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Vault is provided by Ether LLC. This policy explains how the app handles data.

## Data stored by the app

Vault stores the notes you create, including their titles, text, optional tags and
summaries, and dates, in the app’s local SwiftData store on your device. Tags and
summaries produced with the assistant are stored only after you review and confirm
the proposed changes. The app does not include an analytics SDK, advertising SDK,
account system, remote content database, or photo-library integration.

## On-device assistant

On supported Apple devices and languages, assistant requests use Apple Foundation
Models on device. Vault does not send prompts or generated responses to a remote
AI provider or to Ether LLC. Model availability and system-level diagnostics are
controlled by the operating system and Apple’s applicable terms.

## Purchases

Apple processes purchases. Vault requests localized product information and reads
cryptographically verified StoreKit transactions to decide whether Pro access is
active. Ether LLC does not receive or store your payment-card details in the app.

## Retention and deletion

Local content remains on the device until you delete it in Vault, remove the app,
or erase the device. Deleting an item removes it from the app’s local store.
StoreKit transaction history is retained by Apple under Apple’s policies.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use
[Vault Support](https://ether-llc.com/apps/vault/support/). Do not include private
notes, payment information, or other sensitive data in a support request.

This policy may change when Vault adds a new data practice. The effective date
above will be updated when that happens.
